How to use it well

  1. Take it from the official source only (above), then verify the checksum.
  2. Update when the maintainers publish an advisory for your version, not on every release.
  3. If you use AI tools or MCP servers with it, point them at one folder only.
  4. Check what the app can access on your computer before you give it more.
  5. To remove it cleanly, uninstall with the app's own uninstaller and delete its folder.
